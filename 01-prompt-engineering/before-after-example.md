# Before and After Prompt Example

## Workplace Scenario

As a Business Analyst, I often need to turn unclear requests into structured and useful information for stakeholders.

## Before — Weak Prompt

Write a summary of this meeting.

## Initial Result

The result may be too general and may not clearly identify the decisions, requirements, or next steps from the meeting.

## After — Improved Prompt

Act as a Business Analyst. Review the meeting notes I provide and create a structured summary. Identify the key decisions, business requirements, concerns, and next steps. Use clear headings and bullet points. Do not invent information that is not included in the notes. If information is missing, write [Not Specified].

## Improved Result

### Key Decisions
- [Not Specified]

### Business Requirements
- [Not Specified]

### Concerns
- [Not Specified]

### Next Steps
- [Not Specified]

## What Improved?

- The role of the AI is clearly defined.
- The task and expected output are specific.
- The prompt prevents the AI from inventing missing information.
- The improved structure makes the result easier for a Business Analyst to review and use.
