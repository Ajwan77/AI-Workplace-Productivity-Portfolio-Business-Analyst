# Responsible AI — Business Analyst

## Accuracy

AI-generated information should be checked against reliable source information before it is used in business decisions.

## Accountability

The Business Analyst remains responsible for reviewing AI-generated content and deciding whether it is appropriate to use.

## Confidentiality

Confidential or sensitive organizational information should not be entered into AI tools or uploaded to a public GitHub repository.

## Privacy

Personal or private information should be protected. Examples used in this portfolio are fictional or anonymized.

## Bias

AI outputs should be reviewed for possible bias or unfair assumptions, especially when the output could affect people or business decisions.

## Human Oversight

AI can support analysis and organization, but important decisions should remain under human review and approval.

## Green / Amber / Red Classification

| Information Item | Classification | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Fictional meeting notes | Green | No real personal or confidential information is included. |
| Anonymized business requirements | Amber | It can be used for analysis after checking that no sensitive information is included. |
| Real employee personal information | Red | Personal information should not be uploaded or shared with AI or a public repository. |

## Responsible Use Decision

For this portfolio, I use fictional or anonymized information only. AI-generated content is reviewed before inclusion, and important decisions remain under human oversight.
