# Planning Workflow — Business Analyst

## Workplace Scenario

As a Business Analyst, I use AI to help structure work plans while keeping the final decisions and priorities under human control.

## Goal

Improve the customer onboarding process by identifying the main issues and organizing the improvement work into clear phases.

## Mechanisms

- Review the current onboarding process.
- Collect information about customer delays.
- Identify bottlenecks and unnecessary steps.
- Organize findings into actionable improvements.
- Review proposed improvements with relevant stakeholders.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Understand the Current Process
- Collect available process information.
- Document the current onboarding steps.
- Identify areas that require further investigation.

### Phase 2 — Analyze Issues
- Review reported customer delays.
- Identify possible bottlenecks.
- Separate confirmed information from assumptions.

### Phase 3 — Develop Improvements
- Identify possible process improvements.
- Prioritize improvements based on their potential impact.
- Document open questions.

### Phase 4 — Review and Decide
- Present findings to stakeholders.
- Review proposed improvements.
- Agree on the next steps.

## Tasks

| Phase | Task | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Understand | Document current onboarding steps | High |
| Analyze | Identify potential bottlenecks | High |
| Analyze | Review customer delay information | Medium |
| Improve | Develop improvement suggestions | Medium |
| Review | Present findings to stakeholders | High |

## Human Decision

AI is used to structure the plan and organize tasks. Final priorities, decisions, and actions remain the responsibility of the Business Analyst and relevant stakeholders.
