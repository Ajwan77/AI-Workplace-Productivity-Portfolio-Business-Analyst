# Professional Writing Workflow — Business Analyst

## Workplace Scenario

As a Business Analyst, I may need to communicate project updates clearly to stakeholders.

## AI-Generated Draft

Subject: Project Update

Hello,

I wanted to provide an update about the project. We have made progress and there are some items that still need to be reviewed.

Please let me know if you have any questions.

Best regards,
Business Analyst

## Fact Verification

I checked the draft to make sure that:
- No unsupported facts or numbers were added.
- No specific deadlines were invented.
- The message does not claim that an action was completed unless it was provided.
- Missing information remains unspecified.

## Tone Refinement

I refined the message to make it:
- Clear and concise.
- Professional and appropriate for stakeholders.
- Easy to understand.
- Neutral without making unsupported claims.

## Human Review / Sign-off

I reviewed the AI-generated content and confirmed that it is appropriate for the intended workplace context. I take responsibility for the final version.

## Final Version

**Subject: Project Update**

Hello,

I am writing to share a brief update on the project. The current information has been reviewed, and the remaining items require further follow-up.

Please let me know if you have any questions or if additional clarification is needed.

Best regards,  
Business Analyst
