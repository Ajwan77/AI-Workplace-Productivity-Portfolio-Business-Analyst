# Personal AI Integration Plan — Business Analyst

## Purpose

I plan to use generative AI as a productivity assistant for recurring Business Analyst tasks while keeping verification and final decisions under human control.

## Recurring Tasks

| Task | Expected Benefit | Preferred Prompt / Workflow | Risk / Verification | Frequency | Success Looks Like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meeting summaries | Save time organizing notes | Use a structured C.A.R.E. prompt to summarize decisions, requirements, and next steps. | Check the summary against the original notes. | After meetings | Clear and accurate summaries are produced faster. |
| Requirements organization | Make requirements easier to review | Ask AI to categorize requirements and identify open questions. | Verify that no requirements were invented or changed. | Weekly / when needed | Requirements are organized and easier to analyze. |
| Stakeholder communication | Improve clarity and consistency | Use AI to create a professional first draft, then review and refine it. | Verify facts, names, dates, and tone before sending. | When needed | Professional messages require less drafting time. |

## Human Oversight

AI will support repetitive and organizational tasks, but I will remain responsible for reviewing the output and making final decisions.

## Responsible Use

I will not use confidential, private, or sensitive organizational information in public AI tools or this portfolio. I will use fictional or anonymized examples when necessary.

## Success Criteria

My AI use will be successful if it:
- Reduces time spent on repetitive tasks.
- Produces clearer and more organized information.
- Helps me focus more on analysis and decision support.
- Maintains accuracy through human verification.
