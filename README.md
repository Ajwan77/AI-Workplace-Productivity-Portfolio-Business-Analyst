## Project Overview

This portfolio demonstrates how generative AI can support common Business Analyst tasks and improve workplace productivity. It brings together practical examples from the training labs, including prompt engineering, professional writing, information processing, planning, verification, and responsible AI use.

The portfolio focuses on using AI as a productivity assistant while keeping human review and decision-making at the center of the workflow.

## Selected Workplace Scenario

The selected workplace scenario is a Business Analyst. The examples in this portfolio show how generative AI can support a Business Analyst in organizing information, summarizing meetings, preparing professional communication, structuring requirements, planning work, and supporting business decisions.

## Key Learnings

1. A well-structured prompt produces clearer and more useful AI output.
2. AI-generated content should be reviewed and verified before being used.
3. AI can help organize information and plans, but important decisions should remain under human oversight.
4. Responsible AI use requires attention to accuracy, privacy, confidentiality, bias, and accountability.
5. AI is most useful when it supports human work rather than replacing human judgment.
