# Information Processing Workflow — Business Analyst

## Workplace Scenario

As a Business Analyst, I need to turn unstructured information into a clear format that supports business decisions.

## Input — Raw Meeting Notes

The team discussed improving the customer onboarding process.

Some customers are experiencing delays.

The operations team suggested reviewing the current process.

The team agreed that the process needs further analysis.

A follow-up discussion will be needed.

## Prompt

Act as a Business Analyst. Review the meeting notes and transform them into a decision-ready brief. Extract the main issue, key observations, action items, and open questions. Do not invent missing information. If a detail is not provided, write [Not Specified].

## AI Output

### Main Issue
Customer onboarding may be experiencing delays.

### Key Observations
- Some customers are experiencing delays.
- The current onboarding process needs further analysis.

### Action Items
| Action Item | Owner | Deadline | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Review the current onboarding process | [Not Specified] | [Not Specified] | [Not Specified] | [Not Specified] |

### Open Questions
- What is causing the onboarding delays?
- Who is responsible for reviewing the process?
- When should the review be completed?

## Human Review

I reviewed the AI output against the original notes. I removed any information that was not directly supported by the source and kept missing details as [Not Specified].
